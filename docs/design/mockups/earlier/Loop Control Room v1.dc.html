Pilot mode

How much the loop and Helix decide without you. Builders always run inside their sandboxes; this only governs plan changes, retries and answers.
You're at the desk. Helix brings you HITL requests as they come up; rate each one and it learns what to stop asking.
Cautious · if in doubt, ask. Everything in Prudent, plus whenever a builder isn't sure.
Prudent · everything in Intrepid, plus scope creep, architecture changes, and compromises a constraint forced.
Intrepid · only high-risk items: repeated failures, security, architectural impact.
You're away. The loop keeps building and Helix resolves what it can.
Intrepid · resolves everything except high-risk items, which park their task until you're back. The rest of the loop keeps going.
YOLO · resolves everything itself. Releases and approvals still wait for you.
